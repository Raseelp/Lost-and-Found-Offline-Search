The face detector and the face recognition model

The face detector is SCRFD-2.5G (det_2.5g). The face recognition model is MobileFaceNet (w600k_mbf), trained on
WebFace600K. Both come from InsightFace, https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface.

InsightFace publishes its code under the MIT License. It says that its pretrained models, and the data they were
trained on, are for non-commercial research use only.

Lost & Found is free, has no ads and takes no payment. Anyone who builds a commercial product from its code must
check the terms of these models first.
