Privacy Policy

Lost & Found: Offline Search (package: dev.raseel.lostandfound)
Effective date: 11 November 2026
Developer: Raseel P, an individual developer, publishing on Google Play as The Firefly

Lost & Found: Offline Search works on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own. It does not send your photos, videos, faces, searches or any other personal data to the developer or to anyone else. The only thing it downloads from the internet is its search model, once, on first launch.

What the app does with your photos and videos

To let you search by describing a photo, the app reads the photos and videos you give it access to and makes a list of numbers (an embedding) for each one. When you search, your words are turned into numbers the same way and compared with those lists.

To group photos by person, the app finds faces in your photos and videos and makes a list of numbers for each face. Faces that match are grouped into a person. If you give a person a name, the name is saved too. The app also saves the collections you create, the folders you chose to index, the dates of your photos, and its own settings and reminder state.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. No other app can read it, and the app does not upload it. The lists of numbers are not pictures. You can clear the search index and the face data in Settings at any time, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

What the app downloads

On first launch the app downloads two model files (about 104 MB together) from the project's release page on GitHub (github.com). Like any download, this lets GitHub see your IP address and that the file was requested. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that. No photos and no personal data are sent. After the download the app works fully offline. The face models are part of the app and need no download.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Photos and videos: to build the search index and show your results. The app only reads them on your phone.
  • Notifications: to show scan progress and, if you leave them on, the daily memory, the stories note and the reminder to finish indexing. Each can be turned off in Settings.
  • Foreground service and keeping the phone awake: so a long scan of a big library can finish while the app is in the background. A notification is shown the whole time.
  • Internet and network state: only for the one-time model download.
  • Set wallpaper, and storage on older Android versions: only for the viewer's Set as wallpaper and Save a copy actions.

Sharing and export

When you share a photo, save a copy, set it as your wallpaper or export a zip file, the file goes where you tell it: another app you pick in the share menu, your Downloads folder, or your wallpaper. The developer does not receive these files.

Logs

The app keeps short diagnostic lines on your phone. They are only copied to the clipboard when you tap Copy logs in Settings, and it is up to you whether to send them to anyone.

Third-party software

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries. It uses open-source software that runs on your phone (Flutter, ONNX Runtime and Android libraries) and open models: Apple MobileCLIP for search, and SCRFD and MobileFaceNet from InsightFace for faces. If you have opted in on your device, Google Play may give the developer anonymous crash and install statistics. Those are collected by Google, not by the app.

Children

The app is meant for adults, 18 and over, and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.
